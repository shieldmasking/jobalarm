Built for the 2026 Joint Commission Standard

Effective January 1, 2026, the Joint Commission's National Performance Goal 12 requires hospitals to demonstrate that staffing decisions are based on documented patient acuity. AcuityCommand was designed from the ground up to meet this standard.

  • Acuity ratios (1:3, 1:4, 1:5) captured per shift interval and stored for surveyor review
  • Documented evidence that staffing adjustments respond to real-time acuity changes
  • Leadership escalation trails proving nurse executive oversight of staffing decisions
  • Trend reports showing staffing adequacy patterns over time — exactly what NPG 12 audits
