Platform Features

Intelligence at every level of nursing leadership

From charge nurse to CNO — the right information at the right time.

Short-Interval Staffing Reports

Charge nurses submit structured reports on a defined interval. Leadership gets a live view of every unit without picking up the phone.

Acuity Ratio Tracking

Patient counts by acuity ratio (1:3, 1:4, 1:5) are captured every interval and stored. Surveyors can see documented proof that staffing matched patient complexity.

Escalation Alerts to Leadership

Staffing thresholds trigger automatic escalations. The right manager is notified immediately — and every alert, response, and resolution is logged.

Safety Huddle & Task Management

Digital safety huddles with structured documentation. Task creation, assignment, and closure — all tracked and visible to nursing leadership in real time.

Your compliance audit trail is already built
