Platform Features

Designed around the charge nurse workflow

Charge nurses drive data collection. Leadership gets the visibility. Everyone wins.

01 — Staffing Reports

Short-Interval Reports by Charge Nurses

Structured staffing snapshots submitted throughout each shift by your charge nurses. No freeform notes — consistent, comparable data that builds a real-time view of every unit for leadership.

02 — Acuity Tracking

Patient Acuity Ratios Per Interval

Patient counts at each acuity tier (1:3, 1:4, 1:5) are captured every reporting interval. This creates the timestamped, unit-level acuity record that Joint Commission NPG 12 requires — automatically.

03 — Escalations

Automated Leadership Escalation Alerts

When staffing crosses a threshold, the right people are notified immediately. Escalation history — who was notified, when, and how it was resolved — is retained in full for leadership review.

04 — Huddle & Tasks

Safety Huddle & Task Management

Digital safety huddles with structured documentation. Tasks created during rounds or huddles are assigned, tracked to completion, and visible across all units — no more lost follow-ups.

Joint Commission NPG 12 — We Built for It

The 2026 National Performance Goal 12 requires hospitals to document that staffing decisions are grounded in patient acuity. ProductivRN creates that documentation as a natural byproduct of your existing charge nurse workflow — no extra steps, no manual reports to assemble for surveyors.
