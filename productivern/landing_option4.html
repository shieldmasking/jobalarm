Platform Features

Purpose-built for hospital nursing leadership

Not a generic workforce tool. StaffVision was built specifically for the hospital charge nurse → CNO workflow.

Short-Interval Staffing Reports

Charge nurses submit structured staffing snapshots throughout each shift. Leadership sees a live, hospital-wide view without calling a single unit — real data, every interval.

Acuity Ratio Documentation

Patient counts by acuity tier — 1:3, 1:4, 1:5 — are captured at every interval. Each entry is timestamped and stored, building the acuity audit trail Joint Commission surveyors will require.

Escalation Alerts to Leadership

Automatic alerts when staffing drops below thresholds. The right person is notified in real time. Every escalation — trigger, notification, and resolution — is logged for leadership visibility.

Safety Huddle & Task Tracking

Structured digital safety huddles with full documentation. Tasks are created, assigned, tracked, and closed — visible to all nursing leadership in real time across every unit.
